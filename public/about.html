About this suggestion box

A QR-code suggestion box that replaces the box on the wall. Scan the code, write a message or attach a file, voice note or video, choose who should read it, and follow whether it was seen and answered.

How it protects you

Suggestions are anonymous by default. A contact is optional, stored encrypted, and never shown to the reader. Your tracking code is kept hashed, so only you hold it. No IP addresses are stored, and emails never contain your message.

Built in Uganda

Made in Uganda by BrytMa Tech Uganda, for organisations that want to hear from their people without the paperwork.
